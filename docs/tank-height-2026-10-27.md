# Tank height support — 2026-10-27

The tank graphic, animated feet conversion, pending-pull drain, current/future estimates and planning sliders now use the well catalogue's saved `tankHeight`. Older missing/invalid heights retain a 20-foot fallback. Both 20- and 25-foot tanks can be planned through their physical full height; the previous fixed 18-foot planning limit is removed. Summary carries each height into its projections and allows its shared slider up to the tallest displayed tank. Targets above a shorter tank's height have no ETA rather than an impossible predicted time. Saved slider values clamp when tank height or displayed scope shrinks. Frozen/down tanks rescale after a height edit without adding production. Record Load's time-picker estimate also uses the configured height. No readings, loads or tank records are rewritten.
