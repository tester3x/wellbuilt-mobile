# Well swipe transition repair, October 27

User reported jerky/fast well-to-well water motion and a destination level appearing before its grow/shrink. Source started at c487ab6, retaining the prior combined desktop/laptop features and test/typecheck repairs. No build requested.

The activation effect started an 800 ms transition from a mutable previous-well lookup. Loading depended on activation and could replace that with a 400 ms snapshot animation; the live estimate could also replace it with a 500 ms animation. Offscreen cards were preloaded at their own destination fraction, exposing it before the 60-percent activation threshold.

The parent now captures the departing well's displayed fraction at drag start and before swipe/picker selection changes. Offscreen cards stage at that captured fraction. useWellLevelTransition is the single ordinary level-animation owner: entry grows/shrinks for 1000 ms with in/out easing; accepted refresh/live targets arriving during entry are deferred until entry finishes, then continue from the drawn value. Activation no longer reloads storage or supplies a competing animation. Pending-pull drain animations retain authority. Stale completion callbacks cannot retarget a later entry. Delayed data, reduced motion and independent mounted tanks are supported. Measurement/AFR/projection calculations and tank-height normalization are unchanged.

An actual screen-callback test verifies capture uses the departing displayed frame rather than destination preload. Height, down-snapshot and dual-card wiring tests follow the new target/animation separation.
